On June 8, at WWDC, Apple previewed changes to its parental controls for iOS 27, iPadOS 27 and macOS 27. The announcement described a fall release after summer beta testing. Several features address tasks parents have had to manage manually.

## The announced features

**Ask to Browse** brings an approval request to Safari, similar to Ask to Buy for App Store downloads. On a child account, opening a new website for the first time pauses access and sends the parent a request. Apple announced this for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

**Contact approvals** let parents manage who a child talks to through Messages, FaceTime and Phone, starting at setup. Children can be required to ask before connecting with someone new.

**Communication Safety** already blurs nude images in Messages and FaceTime and is on by default for under-18s. Apple said it would expand to detect and block gore and violent content in shared images and videos. The analysis runs on the device without sending the child's photos to Apple.

**Time Allowances** provide separate limits for categories such as Entertainment, Games and Social Media, with age-based starting suggestions drawn from expert research. Schedules can make selected apps unavailable during school or at night.

**The redesigned Screen Time** shows average usage and most-used apps, with one-tap options to pause the device or extend time.

Apple also said it was adapting the American Academy of Pediatrics' Family Media Plan into a guide inside its products. It launched a website for parents and announced an expansion of its global content-reporting tools.

## What this means for other apps

The Declared Age Range API lets an app request a child's age range without receiving their birthday. Developers can use it to adapt the experience. Together with Apple's content-analysis and contact-approval tools for developers, it could extend some protections into other apps.

That depends on developers adopting the tools. Controls for Apple's own apps do not manage TikTok's feed, Discord's servers or Roblox's chat. Check the separate app guides for those settings.

## Plan the setup together

Ask to Browse, contact approvals and category-based Time Allowances can reduce the work parents do manually. You'll still need to agree how to handle requests for a new contact, a website or five more minutes.

Before the first approval request appears, explain what you've enabled and why. Make it easy for your child to ask about a contact or website without feeling that the question itself will get them into trouble.

When the announced features become available in the fall release, review the child account, Ask to Browse, contact approvals and category-based Time Allowances together.
